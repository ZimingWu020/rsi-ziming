What this is

rsi.ziming is Ziming Wu’s personal homepage and blog. It focuses on large-model algorithms, reasoning, and engineering practice — keeping checkable observations as the field moves. It doesn’t chase fleeting trends; it prioritizes thinking, distillation, and hands-on verification.

A quiet garden for research notes: claims should stay revisable, sources stay visible, and practice stays close to judgment.

How to read

Browse by time on the blog list, or start from the featured posts on the home page. Each article ends with comments via Giscus.

Understanding has no perfect tense

“Seed” is intuition. “Growing” is still under revision. “Evergreen” is only temporarily stable. Record facts, mark inferences, keep sources — so later readers, and a future self, can re-check.
