Reading harness: connecting proposals to real execution

2026.10.28 · 6 min · Sample post — replace with your writing

Models are good at proposing the next step. What changes the world is the execution layer. A harness wires proposals, tools, state, and acceptance into a recoverable loop.

A three-way split

Prompt defines intent and constraints. Context decides what this turn can see. Runtime owns calls, waits, failures, and retries. When those blur, debugging collapses into “write another prompt.”

Acceptance evidence

Delivery can’t stop at generated text. Were files written correctly? Did tests pass? Are external side effects idempotent? Those need independent checkers — not model self-grading.

Questions to take away

  • Where does state live, and how do you resume after a crash?
  • Which actions need human approval, and which can run automatically?
  • On failure, can the evidence trail let someone locate the fault in ten minutes?
